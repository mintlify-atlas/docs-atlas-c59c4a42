## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the integration">
    Install Waste Collection Schedule via [HACS](/installation#automated-installation-using-hacs) (recommended) or [manually](/installation#manual-installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your provider">
    Check the [supported providers list](/providers/overview) for your country and waste management company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure a source">
    Add your provider as a source via the Home Assistant Integrations UI or in `configuration.yaml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sensors">
    Create sensors to display next collection dates on your dashboard or trigger automations.
  </Step>
</Steps>
